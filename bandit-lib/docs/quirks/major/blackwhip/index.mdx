# Blackwhip

Manifest energy tendrils from any part of your body and command them at will!

Blackwhip is an extremely versatile quirk. While primarily a utility quirk, it has movement, offensive, and defensive aspects as well.

See **[Abilities](./abilities.mdx)** for every bar skill, and **[Upgrade Tree](./upgrades.mdx)** for every buyable node.

## Ability Bar

Blackwhip abilities occupy **five Palladium ability slots**. Several skills share a slot as modes — hold **Left Alt** (**Ability Mode Select**) to cycle the mode on the slot you are using.

| Slot | Modes |
|------|--------|
| **1** | Tether, Tether (AOE), Detach (Single), Detach (All) |
| **2** | Puppet (Single), Puppet (All) |
| **3** | Manual Swing, Web Swing |
| **4** | Whip Zip, Charge Zip, Block Toss |
| **5** | Lock, Disarm, Auto Pickup, Body Reinforce |

:::tip Starting kit
These come free with the quirk: **Tether**, **Detach (All)**, **Puppet (Single)**, and **Whip Zip**. Everything else is bought with [upgrade points](../../../core-systems/stamina/stamina-progression-and-training.md).
:::

## Core Tether Rules

Blackwhip is a **chain-tether** system. Tips fly out from your body, latch on contact, and stay until you detach them, they snap, or you replace them.

- **Max tethers** starts at **1**. Upgrades raise the cap to 5, and **Quirk Factor** adds more (`QF × 2`).
- **Tether health** starts at **1 heart**. If a chain takes enough damage, it snaps.
- **Grab slots are shared.** Tether, Auto Pickup, and Block Toss all consume the same tether count. Tagged enemies occupy slots that Magnet / Toss would otherwise use.
- **Movement ropes are separate.** Swing, Web Swing, Zip, and Charge Zip use their own movement chains (typically up to 2) and do not steal grab slots the same way.
- **Disarm** fires one independent chain and does not count against your grab cap.
- Chains do not expire on a timer. They last until released, broken, or replaced.
- Chains can be damaged/destroyed by melee attacks, projectiles, and explosions.

## Quirk Factor

**Quirk Factor (QF)** is an attribute (`yha:quirk_factor`) that scales Blackwhip as you grow. At `0` you use the base numbers on each ability. Higher QF adds:

- Extra tethers: `max tethers + (QF × 2)`
- Extra grab range, snap distance, pull, launch, and several damage values

Exact formulas live on the [Abilities](./abilities.mdx) page next to each skill.

Upgrade points are earned by spending [stamina](../../../core-systems/stamina/stamina-system-overview.md) under pressure. See [Stamina Progression and Training](../../../core-systems/stamina/stamina-progression-and-training.md) for how points drop.

## Whip Color

Customize the look of your tendrils. Colors persist and sync to other players. Hex accepts `RRGGBB`, `#RRGGBB`, or `AARRGGBB`.

```
/yha blackwhip color <inner> <glow>
/yha blackwhip color <inner> <outer> <glow>
/yha blackwhip color reset
```

Default canon colors:

| Layer | Hex |
|-------|-----|
| **Inner / core** | `#101A1A` |
| **Outer** | `#25BE9C` |
| **Glow** | `#25BE9C` |
