# Vitality Boost III

An epic gene mutated to provide a higher vitality than the previous generation.

## Gene Information

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Gene ID** | `yha:vitality_boost_3` |
| **Category** | Attribute |
| **Rarity** | Epic |
| **Quality Range** | 30 - 100 |
| **Combinable** | Yes |

## Attribute Effects

| Attribute | Min Modifier | Max Modifier |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Max Health | +11.0 | +16.0 |

## Source Mobs

This gene can be obtained from the following mobs:

- Wither
- Warden

## Combination Recipe

Vitality Boost III can be crafted through gene combination or obtained from mobs. To craft:

### Required Genes

| Gene | Quality Required |
|------|------------------|
| [Vitality Boost I](./vitality-boost-1) | 70+ |
| [Vitality Boost II](./vitality-boost-2) | 80+ |

### Builder Gene Requirements

| Count | Rarity | Minimum Quality |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| 2 | Uncommon | 50+ |

### Success Rate

- **80%** chance of successful combination

## Usage Notes

- Requires BOTH previous vitality genes to craft through combination
- The higher minimum quality threshold (30+) ensures this gene always provides at least +11.0 HP
- Can be extracted from the Wither and Warden, though at very low rates
- Note: Same combination recipe as [Vitality Boost IV](./vitality-boost-4), but with different results
- At maximum quality (100), provides +16.0 maximum health
