# Strength Boost I

A common gene found in many mobs, granting enhanced strength.

## Gene Information

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Gene ID** | `yha:strength_boost_1` |
| **Category** | Attribute |
| **Rarity** | Rare |
| **Quality Range** | 1 - 100 |
| **Combinable** | No |

## Attribute Effects

| Attribute | Min Modifier | Max Modifier |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Attack Damage | +0.5 | +3.0 |

## Source Mobs

This gene can be obtained from the following mobs:

- Iron Golem

## How to Obtain

Strength Boost I can be extracted from Iron Golems using the gene extraction system. The quality of the gene will vary based on the extraction process and any quality modifiers applied.

## Usage Notes

- This is the base-tier strength gene and cannot be combined with other genes
- It serves as the foundation for crafting [Strength Boost II](./strength-boost-2)
- To upgrade, use this gene as an ingredient in the combination recipe for Strength Boost II (requires quality 50+)
