# Strength Boost II

An advanced variant of the strength gene.

## Gene Information

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Gene ID** | `yha:strength_boost_2` |
| **Category** | Attribute |
| **Rarity** | Rare |
| **Quality Range** | 1 - 100 |
| **Combinable** | Yes |

## Attribute Effects

| Attribute | Min Modifier | Max Modifier |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Attack Damage | +3.0 | +5.0 |

## Combination Recipe

Strength Boost II is crafted through gene combination. You will need:

### Required Gene

| Gene | Quality Required |
|------|------------------|
| [Strength Boost I](./strength-boost-1) | 50+ |

### Builder Gene Requirements

| Count | Rarity | Minimum Quality |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Uncommon | 30+ |

### Success Rate

- **75%** chance of successful combination

## Usage Notes

- This gene provides significantly more attack damage than Strength Boost I
- Can be further combined to create [Strength Boost III](./strength-boost-3)
- The higher minimum modifier (+3.0 vs +0.5) ensures consistent damage output even at lower qualities
