## Understanding Attribute Genes

### How They Work

Attribute genes modify your base Minecraft attributes:

- **Attack Damage** - Increases the damage dealt by melee attacks
- **Attack Speed** - Increases the speed of melee attacks
- **Max Health** - Increases your maximum health points (HP)

### Quality Scaling

Each attribute gene has a quality value (1-100) that determines the strength of its effect within the min/max modifier range. Higher quality genes provide stronger bonuses.

### Combining Attribute Genes

Some attribute genes are combinable, allowing you to upgrade them to higher-tier versions through the gene combination system. Check individual gene pages for combination recipes and requirements.
