# Vitality Boost I

An uncommon gene extracted from the brutes of this world, granting enhanced health.

## Gene Information

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Gene ID** | `yha:vitality_boost_1` |
| **Category** | Attribute |
| **Rarity** | Uncommon |
| **Quality Range** | 1 - 100 |
| **Combinable** | No |

## Attribute Effects

| Attribute | Min Modifier | Max Modifier |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Max Health | +1.0 | +6.0 |

## Source Mobs

This gene can be obtained from the following mobs:

- Iron Golem
- Ravager
- Wither
- Warden

## How to Obtain

Vitality Boost I can be extracted from powerful mobs known for their durability. Iron Golems and Ravagers are the most accessible sources, while the Wither and Warden provide guaranteed high-quality extractions.

## Usage Notes

- This is the base-tier vitality gene and cannot be combined with other genes
- It serves as the foundation for crafting [Vitality Boost II](./vitality-boost-2)
- To upgrade, use this gene as an ingredient in the combination recipe for Vitality Boost II (requires quality 70+)
- Also required for higher-tier vitality genes (Boost III and IV)
