# Strength Boost III

A high-end strength variant requiring multiple builder rarities.

## Gene Information

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Gene ID** | `yha:strength_boost_3` |
| **Category** | Attribute |
| **Rarity** | Epic |
| **Quality Range** | 1 - 100 |
| **Combinable** | Yes |
| **Combination Only** | Yes (cannot be obtained from mobs) |

## Attribute Effects

| Attribute | Min Modifier | Max Modifier |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Attack Damage | +5.0 | +8.5 |
| Attack Speed | +0.05 | +0.2 |

## Combination Recipe

Strength Boost III is crafted through gene combination. You will need:

### Required Gene

| Gene | Quality Required |
|------|------------------|
| [Strength Boost II](./strength-boost-2) | 65+ |

### Builder Gene Requirements

| Count | Rarity | Minimum Quality |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Common | 40+ |
| 1 | Rare | 70+ |

### Success Rate

- **55%** chance of successful combination

## Usage Notes

- This is currently the highest-tier strength gene available
- **Combination Only**: This gene cannot be extracted from mobs and must be crafted
- Provides both increased attack damage AND attack speed, making it a dual-attribute gene
- The complex recipe requiring two different builder rarities reflects its power
- At maximum quality (100), provides +8.5 attack damage and +0.2 attack speed
