# Vitality Boost IV

The perfected version of the vitality gene, granting extreme max health.

## Gene Information

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Gene ID** | `yha:vitality_boost_4` |
| **Category** | Attribute |
| **Rarity** | Legendary |
| **Quality Range** | 100 - 100 (Always perfect quality) |
| **Combinable** | Yes |
| **Ignore Player DNA** | Yes |

## Attribute Effects

| Attribute | Min Modifier | Max Modifier |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Max Health | +15.0 | +25.0 |

## Source Mobs

This gene can be obtained from the following mobs:

- Wither
- Warden

## Combination Recipe

Vitality Boost IV can be crafted through gene combination or obtained from mobs. To craft:

### Required Genes

| Gene | Quality Required |
|------|------------------|
| [Vitality Boost I](./vitality-boost-1) | 70+ |
| [Vitality Boost II](./vitality-boost-2) | 80+ |

### Builder Gene Requirements

| Count | Rarity | Minimum Quality |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| 2 | Uncommon | 50+ |

### Success Rate

- **80%** chance of successful combination

## Special Properties

### Ignore Player DNA

This gene has the `ignorePlayerDNA` flag set to `true`. This means:
- The gene's effects apply regardless of the player's DNA compatibility
- Useful for players who haven't fully developed their genetic profile
- Bypasses normal DNA restriction checks

### Perfect Quality

Vitality Boost IV always has quality 100, meaning:
- You always get the maximum +25.0 health bonus
- No quality variation - every copy is identical
- The gene is always at its peak potential

## Usage Notes

- This is the ultimate vitality gene, providing massive health bonuses
- Always quality 100, so the modifier is always +25.0 max health
- The same combination recipe as [Vitality Boost III](./vitality-boost-3) - results may vary
- Can be extracted from the Wither and Warden at extremely low rates
- The Legendary rarity makes this one of the most difficult genes to obtain in the game
