# Vitality Boost II

A rare gene mutated to provide a higher vitality than the previous generation.

## Gene Information

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Gene ID** | `yha:vitality_boost_2` |
| **Category** | Attribute |
| **Rarity** | Rare |
| **Quality Range** | 1 - 100 |
| **Combinable** | Yes |

## Attribute Effects

| Attribute | Min Modifier | Max Modifier |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Max Health | +6.0 | +11.0 |

## Source Mobs

This gene can be obtained from the following mobs:

- Iron Golem
- Ravager
- Wither
- Warden

## Combination Recipe

Vitality Boost II can be crafted through gene combination or obtained from mobs. To craft:

### Required Gene

| Gene | Quality Required |
|------|------------------|
| [Vitality Boost I](./vitality-boost-1) | 70+ |

### Builder Gene Requirements

| Count | Rarity | Minimum Quality |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Uncommon | 50+ |

### Success Rate

- **75%** chance of successful combination

## Usage Notes

- Can be extracted from the same mobs as Vitality Boost I, but at lower rates due to its Rare rarity
- Provides a significant health boost with a minimum of +6.0 HP even at quality 1
- Can be further combined to create [Vitality Boost III](./vitality-boost-3) and [Vitality Boost IV](./vitality-boost-4)
- At maximum quality (100), provides +11.0 maximum health
