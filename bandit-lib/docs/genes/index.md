# Genes

This section documents every gene in the mod. (There are a lot)

## Category Overview

Genes are organized into different categories based on their function:

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Builder** | Genes used as crafting components to create other genes. These typically don't have active effects on their own but serve as building blocks for more complex genetic modifications. |
| **Attribute** | Genes that provide passive stat bonuses to your character, such as increased strength or vitality. |
| **Resistance** | Genes that grant immunity or resistance to various damage types and environmental hazards. |

## Rarity Overview

All genes in the mod have a rarity tier that determines their quality range and availability:

| Rarity | Quality Range | Description |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| **Common** | 1 - 100 | Basic genes with the widest quality variation. Easy to find but may have lower overall quality. |
| **Uncommon** | 20 - 100 | Better minimum quality threshold. Slightly harder to obtain than common genes. |
| **Rare** | 40 - 100 (or 1 - 100 for some) | Mid-tier genes with good minimum quality. Some rare genes have specific crafting uses. |
| **Epic** | 60 - 100 | High minimum quality, valuable for crafting and use. Significantly harder to obtain. |
| **Legendary** | 100 - 100 | Maximum quality only. Extremely rare and powerful. |

## Usage Notes

- **Quality Range**: The quality range shows the minimum and maximum quality values a gene can have when obtained. Higher quality genes are more effective.
- **Combinable**: Some genes can be combined with others to create hybrid effects. Check individual gene pages for combinable status.
- **Source Mobs**: Many genes can only be obtained from specific mobs. Check individual gene tables for mob sources. If the value is left blank, it means any mob can spawn with that gene.
- **Crafting**: Builder genes are primarily used as crafting components for creating more advanced genes and modifications.
